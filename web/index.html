How it works

This application analyzes audio signals to create a 3D geometric representation of your song. It breaks the audio into small time segments and extracts spectral features (frequency content) for each segment.

The 3D Construction

Analysis: The audio is processed using Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to determine the energy at different frequencies.

Mapping: We use dimensionality reduction (like PCA or t-SNE concepts) to map these high-dimensional frequency features into 3D space (X, Y, Z). Segments that sound similar will appear closer together in space, forming clusters and structures that represent the "shape" of the song's timbre.

Controls

  • Mapping Mode: Switch between structural (Manifold) and chronological (Time Spine) views.
  • Refine Geometry: Adjust the X/Y/Z position of the point cloud.
  • Visibility: Use "Nodes Only" to hide edges and effects for a cleaner view.
